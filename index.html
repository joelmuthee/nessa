About Nessa

Vanessa is a Nairobi-based makeup artist and wardrobe stylist. Her client list reads like a Kenyan TVC reel: Premier Bet, Farmer's Choice, Safaricom, M-Pesa, Absa, Ushindi, Detrex, Galito's, Dettol.

In 2022 she was nominated for the Kalasha Award for Best Costume Designer for her work on screen. When she's not on a shoot, she runs ThriftLux, a hand-picked pre-loved designer handbag shop delivering across Kenya.
