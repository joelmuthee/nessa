About ThriftLux

ThriftLux curates pre-loved and quality-reviewed handbags for the modern woman in Nairobi who wants the look without the markup. Every bag is hand-picked, photographed, and listed once it's been checked over.

We're not affiliated with any brand. We just love beautiful bags.

How to buy

  1. Tap Enquire on the bag you love. It opens WhatsApp with the bag pre-filled.
  2. Confirm availability and Venessa will share the M-Pesa details on WhatsApp.
  3. Pick up in Nairobi CBD, or we can arrange delivery anywhere in the country.
